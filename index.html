The Problem

Turn historical support knowledge into intelligent recommendations.

IT organizations accumulate enormous amounts of troubleshooting knowledge inside resolved tickets. Finding the right solution manually can be slow and inconsistent.

⚠️ Traditional Workflow

Support engineers manually search historical tickets, documentation and previous incidents.

  • Large ticket volumes
  • Inconsistent terminology
  • Slow knowledge discovery
  • Repeated troubleshooting effort
  • Loss of organizational knowledge

🚀 Intelligent Workflow

The recommendation engine retrieves and ranks historically relevant resolutions for each new ticket.

  • Automatic ticket understanding
  • Historical resolution retrieval
  • Ranked recommendations
  • Top-K evaluation
  • Human-in-the-loop decision support

Experimental Results

Strong retrieval and ranking performance.

The evaluation focuses on whether the correct historical resolution is surfaced among the highest-ranked recommendations.

Metric Score Meaning
Recall@1 0.890 Correct resolution appears as the first recommendation in 89% of evaluated cases.
Recall@5 0.970 Correct resolution appears within the top five recommendations in 97% of cases.
MRR@5 0.930 Indicates strong ranking quality across the top five results.

Research Methodology

A reproducible experimental pipeline.

01 · Dataset Generation

Generate a controlled ticket-resolution dataset using a fixed random seed for reproducible experiments.

02 · Text Processing

Normalize and prepare ticket text for downstream NLP representation.

03 · Representation

Transform ticket descriptions into representations suitable for retrieval and similarity comparison.

04 · Retrieval & Ranking

Retrieve historical candidates and rank their associated resolutions according to relevance.

05 · Evaluation

Measure recommendation quality using Recall@1, Recall@5 and MRR@5.
